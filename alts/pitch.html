Find

It finds the fund's own filing

The N-2, S-11 or 10-K, straight from the SEC. Exit terms and fees are rarely summarized up front, so it goes looking where they actually live.

Read

Down to the clause that matters

Repurchase limits, suspension rights and the risks specific to this fund, not the boilerplate. An afternoon per prospectus becomes a few minutes for the first pass.

Extract

Every term, with a page number

Liquidity terms and each fee layer, pulled out and cited. The concern level it gives is a read on the terms, never a buy or sell signal.

Log

Logged with every page it cited

The read and each page number it relied on, so anyone can check the work against the filing itself.

Check facts

Then every term is checked

Every fee, limit and date is checked against the fund's own filing, and anything that doesn't match is fixed. Then a person reviews the read before it's sent.

Repeated misses become lessons for the next read.
