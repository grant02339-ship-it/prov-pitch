Read

A name comes in, and it reads

News and analyst coverage from the last ten days, and the company's actual SEC filing rather than a summary of it. That's the hour or two per name this replaces. Your judgment on it isn't.

Check

It looks for what changed

Guidance, debt, insider activity and analyst revisions, each tied to the line it came from. For a name under real consideration, it also runs seven independently checkable criteria.

Write

Turned into one written read

What changed, what it means, and the actual sources used. No buy or sell call. The reading and writing is where the AI works. The judgment isn't.

Log

Every source is recorded

The read and each source behind it, dated and labeled, so you can trace any line back to where it came from.

Check facts

Then every fact is checked

Every number, date and quote is checked against its original source, and anything a source contradicts is fixed. Then a person reviews the read before it's sent.

Its sources are graded too, and repeated misses become lessons for the next read, in a record you can audit yourself.

How working together goes

  1. We scope it

    A short call sets exactly what gets covered: a watchlist, a set of filings, one real question. Agreed before anything starts.

  2. Done for you, from public sources

    We run the research on public information: filings, company releases, news and trade press. You get the finished report, with nothing to install or learn.

  3. You get a written report

    What it found, what cuts against it, and every source it read. No buy or sell call. One section is left blank for your own judgment.

  4. NDA first, always

    Signed before we see anything specific about your watchlist or your names. No exceptions.

  5. You decide what's next

    Act on it, ignore it, or come back with a follow-up. The report is yours either way, with no obligation to continue.

What this is, and what it isn't

This is

  • Faster research, plus the checks that keep it honest
  • A way to go deeper on every name, in less time, with the team you already have
  • Every fact tied to the line it came from
  • A method tested on our own work, mistakes included
  • An early partnership. The first clients help shape what this becomes, with direct access to the person building it

This isn't

  • Investment advice, a recommendation, or a sub-advisory arrangement of any kind. Your fiduciary judgment stays yours, on every name
  • Asset management. Your clients' money and your trades stay entirely yours to decide
  • A pitch to cut your team. It's a pitch to get more out of it
  • A one-time pass. Every read's sources are logged and checked, so it keeps getting better at where it looks
