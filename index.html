PROV

Research with full provenance

Every fact traced back to where it came from, checked against that source, and reviewed by a person.

PROV is short for provenance: the record of where information came from and what was done to it. PROV reads the filings, releases and news on a company or fund, checks every number, date and quote, and writes it up. It never tells anyone what to buy or sell.
